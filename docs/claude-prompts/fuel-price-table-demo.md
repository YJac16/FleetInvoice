## Rules (Yaseen, 25 Nov)

Every fill-up (dispatcher, supervisor, or driver) is priced from the table at the **confirmed** rate in force on the slip date; that price then locks and never changes. New rates apply only when slip-date `>= effective_from`. No confirmed rate → `price_status='needs_price'`, money stays **null** (never 0), flag for admin, exclude from invoices until priced. Auto-pickup official rates where possible; admin entry is the fallback. Never hardcode, invent, or seed real prices in migrations; test/demo fixtures must be fake and labelled `DEMO`. Finance history: last locked purchase vs current confirmed rate, cost/km, price trend per vehicle (Drop 2 later — do not build the profitability view). Default region `coastal` (Cape Town). Types (configurable): `diesel_50ppm`, `diesel_500ppm`, `petrol_95`, `petrol_93`. Confirm/enter: `organisation_admin`, `manager`, platform owner (assumed; not confirmed by Yaseen). Backfill of existing unpriced fills is an **opt-in admin action**, not automatic.
